# Make the Xianyu business account the stable commerce owner

Orders, statistics, intake state, business hours, published configuration, and upstream-account binding belong to a stable Xianyu business account owned by one business user, not to a plugin token or installation. One approved plugin token at a time supplies the account's replaceable execution authorization; replacing that token preserves history and configuration while invalidating the old executor. This prevents token renewal, revocation, or device replacement from fragmenting order history and reporting.
